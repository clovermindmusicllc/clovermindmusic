-Whats on my mind today?-

What is on my mind today music lovers? I am thinking about books. What is our opinion on books?. I personally LOVE them. I am constantly reading someting. The pursuit of knowledge is endless. Alongside my love for music I am limitless. Today I picked up a copy of Californication Sheet Music for Bass in a thrift store today for $3 dollars. Three dollars for an album of bass music by Flea. Flea is a killer bassist, simple but great. I know a couple of songs off the album but I wasnt really a big Chillies fan as a kid. Played them alot in bands and what not but never sat down and was like HELL YEAH new chillies album. Anyway back to our topic, books. I currently have a bunch of books on the go about music. One about music buisness, one about receording and studio stuff. Finally I got another book about learning to play guitar for beginners. I was trying to build some new lesson plans for my students. It was an excellent find and I did consider picking up my own copy. Books are super informative and it feels dumb writing that. The way the world is turning these days i felt it needed to be said. The best thing about books is there so many and they are full of opinions. Not like the ones you find online
