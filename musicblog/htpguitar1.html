How to Play Guitar for Wizards!

One

The Begining

The first time I got a Guitar, it was a magical moment. I can't exactly remember if it was Christmas or my birthday, but I remember it came from a toy store. It was a big thick old piece of wood, with a red sunburst effect, the neck was really thick too, and the headstock was kind of similar to a fender telecaster, but it was like bent or point backwards. Yeah it's a little odd. A Burswood from Smyths Toy Store. I've seen so many guitars on TV and was aware of like rock bands like Guns N' Roses, Black Sabbath and nu metal bands like Slipknot and Lamb of God but it was the one the toy store catalog I wanted, it wasn't really till I got one in my hands to call my own that I became obsessed. And I mean, OBSESSED! Listened to every genre that feautred guitar, read the complete history of the guitar, how it was made, the physics behind electric guitars. I wanted to know EVERYTHING.

Obviously my favorite style is gonna be funk music because let’s face it, there is no party without some funky music. That extends into the electronic genres of house music, you’ll find guitar all through the veins of House music, in terms of sound effects or just catchy little licks and riffs. Mostly what shines for me are those groovy chord progressions that you can just shake your bones to all night. One place I find myself coming back to is the Blues. Some consider this to just be the basics, often something we learn about when we have just started guitar lessons. The history behind some of the players has always been fascinating to me mixes well with my love for horror, it's really easy to fall into those stories about selling your soul to the devil just to get better at your instrument.

One thing that always stood out from those stories is how the person thought the instrument was the most important element and if they could master it they could change their lives completely. Now they always wanted to achieve this mastery as quick as possible. Yet in most of those stories the devil forces them to practice at the crossraods, so what does that tell you? Ha! You still gotta practice. The power of music will change your life, that is something I do believe, I honestly approach music like it’s a religion at this point. Without that approach I would not have come as far as I have had today.

Which brings me to the most important part. You. The most magical element of it all. You don’t have to approach music the way I do. You need to figure out what works best for you. What do your days or weeks look like? When can you fit in some practice time? How are you feeling when you are about to start practicing? We often feel guilty and beat ourselves up about not practicing, it almost feels like another chore when we do practice. That is something we definitely want to avoid. You started this because it was cool or fun so lets keep it that way!

Here’s what I recommend:

1) Make a Plan. Whether you map out your day or your week. Make a plan, put a reminder in your calendar or set an alarm on your phone. If you can dedicate at least an hour a week to practicing you will improve and you will be able to track your improvements. Which will in turn fill up your cup of confidence and only inspire you to do more.

2) Keep a Record. Start a journal or keep a folder of everything you have learned or currently learning. Whether its a physical or digital copy. Create someting that you can always have to back you up.

3)Set a Goal. Pick something that you want to be able to do or something you strive to be capable of on the guitar. Get specific whether it is a certain song you want to play or a technique you want to pull off. Give yourself a reasonable deadline and start your mission. Once it is achieved, celebrate it!

Alright now I have given the best three tips I could conjure. Whether you take lessons or are teaching yourself, it is up to you to make the most of it. Put the energy into building on these three foundations and you are flying across the moon. You’ll be looking forward to your next practice session, and excited to find your next challenge. Don't be afraid to get creative with it. Sure, wether you are at home or at the crossroads, the devil can’t save you from praticing.
